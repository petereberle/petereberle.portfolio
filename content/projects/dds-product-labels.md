Developed an automated system for generating client-facing product labels directly from NetSuite. Using SuiteScript and Advanced PDF/HTML templates, the solution enables the sample fulfillment team to print QR-coded labels directly from transaction records, linking each physical sample to current product information online. The workflow reduced issues caused by outdated information on physical tile samples while creating a scalable labeling system for thousands of samples used in commercial project submittals.
